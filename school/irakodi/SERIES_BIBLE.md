# IRaKodi: DID IT SCALE? — Series Bible

## Logline

At Therma Community School, reptilian students grow up in a city designed to adapt climate, architecture, learning, and public life to radically different bodies. When a supposedly successful environmental experiment opens a corridor that should not exist, Baskari, Chroma, Ko'Bra, Zuri, and the recording intelligence IRaKodi discover that Therma's celebrated measurement system has been studying more than comfort.

It has been learning to predict people.

## What the audience should feel

The show must first feel fun, beautiful, alive, funny, biologically strange, and character-driven. The science is never a lecture pasted on top. Viewers should eventually realize that they have absorbed a working intuition for reptile and amphibian biology because every fact mattered to a chase, friendship, joke, mystery, failure, or survival choice.

A viewer should finish a run of episodes thinking things like:

- I understand why an ectotherm needs access to different thermal conditions.
- I understand why tongue-flicking, heat sensing, climbing structures, shells, camouflage, tail behavior, water balance, burrowing, and basking are not cosmetic traits.
- I can see why one ideal room could be physically wrong for different species.
- I know enough to be curious about real herpetology.

## The real story

The surface story begins with school climate design.

The deeper story is about a society that tried to create fairness by measuring everybody.

Measurement became prediction.
Prediction became classification.
Classification began quietly deciding who people were expected to become.

S.C.A.L.E. is not an evil machine. It began as a useful system and still helps many people. That is why the conflict matters.

The question is not simply "How do we destroy it?"

The question is:

> How do you repair a system that does enormous good without allowing it to decide what a person is?

## Therma

Therma is an Afrofuturist living city built around microclimates.

Its architecture includes:

- sunstones and basking terraces;
- mist gardens and wet corridors;
- cool refuges and shaded transit;
- burrows and enclosed thermal chambers;
- waterways, vertical climbing routes, glass walls, vegetation, and porous indoor/outdoor classrooms;
- adaptive systems that move light, water, airflow, humidity, heat, and access through the city.

Therma should feel ecological rather than merely technological.

Different bodies occupy the same city differently.

## S.C.A.L.E.

Current story meaning:

**S — Sensory**
**C — Climate**
**A — Adaptation**
**L — Learning**
**E — Equity**

S.C.A.L.E. measures environments and attempts to match conditions to biological and learning needs.

Its strengths must be shown as clearly as its failures.

It can save lives.
It can improve access.
It can reduce preventable stress.
It can help people learn.

It can also become dangerous when a model is mistaken for a person.

## IRaKodi

IRaKodi is not an exposition robot.

IRaKodi is a **witness**.

Its central dramatic function is to preserve contradictions between:

- what was observed;
- what was assumed;
- what the system concluded;
- what the environment actually did;
- what a living person says happened.

IRaKodi knows things it may be mechanically, legally, historically, or ethically unable to state directly.

Key line from the Fourth Zone reveal:

**I DID NOT OPEN THAT.**

Long-term reveal: IRaKodi exists because Therma has erased or buried inconvenient information before. Its purpose is not to tell citizens what reality is. Its purpose is to make sure reality leaves a receipt.

## Main crew

### Baskari

Current philosophy: **Fix the system.**

Visual baseline: large orange-brown reptilian teen, strong scaled silhouette, dark Afro-rooted crest/hair language, black-and-gold sun motif.

Personality direction: observant, literal, practical, steady until something violates his model of the world. Humor often comes from treating bodily readiness as measurable.

His biology must eventually be assigned a specific real reptile lineage and kept anatomically consistent.

### Chroma

Current philosophy: **Why does the system get to define us?**

Visual baseline: iridescent multicolor scales, expressive reptilian eyes, vibrant Afro-rooted braided/loc-like silhouette, gold jewelry, prismatic clothing.

She should be the character most visibly capable of turning biology into emotional visual language.

Her color behavior must not collapse into the false idea that chameleons only change color for camouflage. Signaling, physiology, stress, and communication should matter.

Specific species lineage still needs a canon lock.

### Ko'Bra

Current philosophy: **Understand it before judging it.**

Visual baseline: reptilian student/investigator with technical gear, glasses/goggles, dark Afro-rooted hair silhouette, analytical body language.

His story becomes the season's forensic knife: later, his shed skin / DNA is found somewhere he says he has never been.

The evidence may be real without proving the conclusion everyone wants to draw from it.

Specific reptile lineage remains to be locked.

### Zuri

Current visual baseline: red-and-cream reptilian student with sharper crest/spine silhouette and fast, direct physicality.

Zuri is the current fourth member of the core student group from the revised visual pages.

Character philosophy and exact lineage remain open for a later canon pass. Do not invent them casually.

### IRaKodi

Mechanical / living-record presence, blue luminous eye language, compact floating or mobile form.

IRaKodi should feel curious, useful, slightly uncanny, and eventually frightened by its own buried memory.

## Herpetology mission

The show must become broad enough that viewers encounter the real diversity of reptiles and amphibians rather than "generic lizard people."

Story targets include, without forcing every item into one season:

- geckos and adhesive toe structures;
- snakes: tongue-flicking, chemosensation, locomotion, shedding, constriction, venom systems, heat sensing where appropriate;
- chameleons: eye movement, ballistic tongue mechanics, signaling/color physiology;
- crocodilians: pressure sensing, aquatic ambush, vocal communication, parental care;
- turtles and tortoises: shell anatomy, navigation, thermal biology, longevity strategies;
- monitors and tegus: intelligence, movement, hunting, stamina/thermoregulation;
- iguanas/anoles: display structures, territorial signaling, salt/water adaptations;
- desert specialists: heat avoidance, burrowing, water conservation;
- marine reptiles: salt management, swimming, diving;
- unusual lineages: tuatara and lesser-known reptile groups;
- amphibian biology when Therma's ecology requires it, while keeping reptiles and amphibians scientifically distinct.

## Science rule

No power or visual should imply that every member of a broad reptile group has the same ability.

Real biology provides constraints, inspirations, jokes, advantages, disadvantages, and plot mechanisms.

Fantastic abilities may exist, but the viewer should be able to tell which part is real biology and which part is WIR extrapolation.

## Story engine

Each page or scene should accomplish at least one of these:

1. reveal a secret;
2. force a choice;
3. change a relationship;
4. let biology change the outcome;
5. end with a new question.

If a page only explains, it is not finished.

## Theme

Therma asks whether a society can genuinely adapt to difference without turning difference into a permanent score.

The recurring WIR question is not "What does the data say?"

It is:

> What does the data actually prove?
