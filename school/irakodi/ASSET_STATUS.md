## Canonization rule

A generated image is not automatically canon.

Before declaring a final production asset, inspect:

- species anatomy;
- character continuity;
- spelling;
- text density;
- WIR stamp;
- page readability at phone size;
- story sequence;
- whether the image reveals/forces/changes something.

The asset vault manifest records source filenames, hashes, dimensions, and GitHub-friendly proofs.
