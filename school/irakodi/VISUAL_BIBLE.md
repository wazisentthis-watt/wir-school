# IRaKodi — WIR Books Visual Bible

## Core look

The target is a more cartoon-forward WIR Books page language:

- bold cel-shaded silhouettes;
- Afro-rooted styling;
- real reptile anatomy pushed into expressive character design;
- neon Afrofuturist climate architecture;
- ancient/future pattern language;
- kinetic street-art cuts;
- halftone / ink energy where useful;
- readable focal hierarchy;
- fewer words;
- more physical storytelling.

The art should be beautiful enough to stop a scroll and clear enough to understand at phone size.

## Herpetologist-proud rule

Characters cannot read as humans with decorative scales.

For every recurring reptilian character, lock a real species or lineage reference before final production and use its anatomy artistically:

- scale size and distribution;
- eyelids / eye shape / pupil behavior;
- jaw structure;
- nostrils;
- neck mobility;
- digit/toe structure;
- claws;
- tail anatomy;
- posture and gait;
- climbing/swimming/burrowing mechanics;
- shedding/skin behavior;
- thermal behavior;
- display structures;
- sensory organs;
- water/salt adaptations where relevant.

Stylization is encouraged. Biological incoherence is not.

## WIR living borders

No default rectangular comic grid.

Borders should be made by the world:

- water;
- mist;
- heat shimmer;
- condensation;
- vines;
- tails;
- shed skin;
- scale patterns;
- sunlight;
- shadow;
- airflow ribbons;
- holographic geometry;
- Action Air Art.

Calm scenes have smooth borders.
Tense scenes become jagged.
Cosmic / hidden-system scenes become warped or geometric.

## WIR word art

Dialogue and narration should usually be embedded into scene material.

No wall of speech bubbles.

Words may appear as:

- condensation on glass;
- heat glow in stone;
- projected diagnostic light;
- scale reflections;
- graffiti;
- woven clothing;
- flowing water;
- vapor;
- claw scratches;
- illuminated piping;
- IRaKodi shell text;
- signage that characters physically occupy.

Text must behave like its material.

Cold text compresses.
Heat text blooms.
Water text refracts.
Fear can fragment.
Sarcasm can curl.
A system message can remain unnervingly clean.

## Word economy

Default target per page:

- one memorable line;
- one useful piece of information;
- one visual question;
- minimal labels.

A page may have six words if six words are enough.

Do not turn the page into a tutorial.

## Action Air Art

Motion leaves environmental evidence before impact.

Use:

- heat trails;
- frost fracture;
- displaced mist;
- tails whipping borders;
- water shear;
- dust;
- scale glints;
- scent/taste currents;
- airflow lines;
- thermal distortion;
- color signaling;
- debris;
- light echoes.

Every action pose is a moment-before:

**wind-up → tension → initiation**

Do not freeze only aftermath.

## WIR show stamp

The show carries a recurring WIR stamp based on the user-supplied translucent octopus reference.

Final mark rule:

- the octopus itself forms the WIR identity;
- tentacles construct the **W**;
- a clear **I** is integrated beneath/through the central body;
- the **R** is formed through the crown/trim/tentacle structure;
- bioluminescent translucent anatomy remains visible;
- it feels alive, not like a flat corporate icon;
- use it as a small consistent stamp on covers/pages/social cuts;
- it may react subtly to the page's palette but the silhouette must stay recognizable.

Reference image is archived in the asset vault.

## Revised cover direction

The best current cover direction is cartoon-forward, high-contrast, vertical, with:

- crew in strong reptilian silhouette;
- Therma Community School;
- IRaKodi;
- the hidden corridor / handprints;
- WIR octopus stamp;
- Action Air Art cuts;
- title **IRaKodi: DID IT SCALE?**

The cover should sell a mystery, not explain a curriculum.

## Visual continuity

Once a character sheet is canonized, do not casually change:

- scale map;
- facial structure;
- crest/hair silhouette;
- tail;
- eye anatomy;
- jewelry;
- clothing motifs;
- body proportions;
- species-specific locomotion.

WIR stylization can exaggerate motion and energy while preserving identity.
