# WIR Books — IRaKodi: DID IT SCALE?

Canonical source folder for the Therma / S.C.A.L.E. reptile-world animated-comic serial.

## Core promise

A WIR mystery-adventure that makes viewers fall in love with reptile biology while the characters discover that a city built to measure and adapt to bodies can also begin predicting, classifying, and limiting them.

The series must work on three meanings of **scale** at the same time:

1. reptile scales;
2. measurement and scoring;
3. whether a system can scale across radically different living bodies.

## Canon status

The early climate-classroom pages remain **visual development**. They established Therma, the climate logic, S.C.A.L.E., the cast silhouette language, and WIR page experiments, but the story was too tutorial-driven.

The revised story direction begins with the later cover / Page 1 reset: **mystery first, character first, herpetology through action, explanation only when the story earns it.**

## Series rule

> No reptile fact enters the story unless somebody needs it, suffers from it, exploits it, misunderstands it, jokes about it, or survives because of it.

WIR visual law remains in force: anticipation frames, wind-up → tension → initiation, living borders, world-embedded dialogue, Afro-rooted design, and Action Air Art.
