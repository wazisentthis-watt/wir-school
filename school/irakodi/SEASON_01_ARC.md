# IRaKodi — Season 1 Arc

## Season question

**Can a system built to adapt to difference avoid turning difference into destiny?**

## Episode 1 — THE FOURTH ZONE

A body-responsive classroom pilot opens an impossible corridor inside Therma Community School. The crew discovers evidence that S.C.A.L.E. has records older than the students it describes.

Hook: IRaKodi already knows the forbidden space.

## Episode 2 — BEFORE YOU WERE BORN

The crew investigates Ko'Bra's pre-birth biological file.

The mystery expands from climate prediction to developmental prediction.

Biology focus: inheritance, development, growth, shedding, environmental influence, and the limits of prediction.

End hook: a predicted trait appears in a student who was never part of the trial.

## Episode 3 — PERFECT FIT

Therma's larger society is revealed.

S.C.A.L.E. routes housing, learning spaces, treatment, transit, and resource recommendations.

Most citizens like it because much of it works.

Biology focus: habitat specialization and the danger of treating preference as destiny.

End hook: Chroma finds a recommendation she was never meant to see.

## Episode 4 — THE SHED

Ko'Bra's shed skin is found somewhere he says he has never been.

The forensic evidence is real.

That does not prove the conclusion.

Biology focus: shedding, skin evidence, DNA persistence, scent/chemical traces, contamination, and inference.

End hook: the sample is newer than Ko'Bra's last known shed.

## Episode 5 — KO'BRA DID IT

The case against Ko'Bra grows.

Friends begin splitting over how much evidence is enough.

Baskari struggles because the numbers are unusually strong.

Chroma asks the season's central forensic question:

**What does the evidence actually prove?**

Biology focus: chemical sensing, identification, behavioral assumptions, and false certainty.

## Episode 6 — THE GOOD NUMBER

The crew meets people whose lives S.C.A.L.E. genuinely saved.

Destroying it would hurt people.

The antagonist is no longer "the machine."

The problem is governance, interpretation, memory, and power.

Biology focus: accessibility, environmental control, health-supporting habitat design, and diversity of need.

## Episode 7 — IRAKODI

IRaKodi's origin is revealed.

It is a continuity witness created because Therma once erased inconvenient information from its own history.

IRaKodi does not exist to tell people reality.

It exists to ensure reality leaves a receipt.

Biology focus: memory in organisms vs memory in systems, archival traces, environmental history.

## Episode 8 — DID IT SCALE?

The hidden trials connect to citywide predictive policy.

The crew must decide whether to expose, disable, repair, or reframe S.C.A.L.E.

The final choice protects the people who rely on adaptive systems while breaking the idea that a score may define a future.

Final reveal:

Ko'Bra's DNA evidence was not planted.

It really is his.

He simply has not done the thing it came from...

**yet.**

Cut to black.

## Season design law

Every episode should introduce new reptile/amphibian biology through a problem that could not play the same way with generic human characters.

The audience comes for the mystery and characters.

The biology is the machinery under the floor.
