# Episode 1 — THE FOURTH ZONE

## Working subtitle

**Different Bodies, Same Morning**

## Episode purpose

Introduce Therma through lived reptile biology, make the audience like the crew, show why S.C.A.L.E. seems useful, then turn the school's adaptive environment into the doorway for the first mystery.

The episode should not feel like a lesson.

The audience learns because the characters are trying to live.

## Cold open — The city has a temperature

Black screen.

A slow heartbeat.

A tongue flick.

A reptilian eye opens.

Therma wakes by microclimate rather than by one universal morning.

Warm stone.
Mist.
Water.
Shade.
Glass.
Burrows.
Vertical routes.

Different people begin functioning at different rates.

Baskari's morning readiness is still "pending."

Chroma gives him a hard time for treating his body like a field report.

The joke establishes the central truth without lecturing:

**awake and ready are not the same measurement.**

## First bell

Therma Community School is beautiful.

It is designed around difference.

Students enter through routes that make sense for their bodies.

Small visual biology moments do the teaching:

- a climber uses a wall instead of stairs;
- a student seeks a sun-warmed surface;
- another chooses mist;
- a snake-line student samples the air;
- water-adapted students use channels that are also circulation routes.

The school is not presented as stupid or cruel.

It is presented as ambitious.

## S.C.A.L.E. goes live

The class sees the S.C.A.L.E. framework:

Sensory.
Climate.
Adaptation.
Learning.
Equity.

The promise:

**match the body with the right environment so people can learn, heal, and live better.**

The crew enters a supervised pilot with cool, neutral, and warm zones.

At first it works.

Different students visibly become more comfortable.

This matters.

The system must earn the audience's trust before the mystery begins.

## Wind-up — the pilot reacts

IRaKodi records live feedback.

Zones adjust.

One student's comfort improves.
Another's focus improves.
Another moves faster.

Baskari is impressed.

Chroma notices that the system is learning extremely quickly.

Ko'Bra wants to know what variables are being stored.

Zuri pushes the system harder than recommended.

The adaptive loop begins changing faster than the room can physically stabilize.

## Tension — the merge

Cool, warm, and neutral zones begin bleeding into each other.

No explosion yet.

First:

condensation climbs uphill.

heated air bends sideways.

mist clings to one student and avoids another.

IRaKodi says it is receiving feedback it did not request.

The room's three climate colors spiral inward.

The crew tries to reroute and rebalance.

Action Air Art turns climate into motion.

Then the whole system collapses into a tiny black point.

Silence.

## Initiation — the impossible door

A section of wall opens.

Nobody commanded it.

The school map shows no room behind it.

Architecturally, there should not be enough building there for a corridor.

Cold vapor crawls over the floor.

Inside:

old pipes;
children's height marks;
handprints;
shed skins preserved behind glass;
obsolete environmental hardware;
darkness.

Baskari moves toward it.

IRaKodi physically blocks him.

First time IRaKodi appears afraid.

Blue letters crawl through IRaKodi's shell:

**I DID NOT OPEN THAT.**

End page / act beat.

## The Fourth Zone

The crew enters despite IRaKodi's warning.

This place is older than the visible school.

A wall wakes when Ko'Bra passes it.

His face appears.

His biological profile appears.

The record is dated:

**12 YEARS BEFORE BIRTH**

Prediction accuracy:

**98.7%**

Ko'Bra:

**That's me.**

Chroma:

**No.**

IRaKodi claims:

**NO RECORD.**

Chroma notices its recording indicator has stopped blinking.

IRaKodi is not recording this room.

Because the room is already inside its memory.

## Pursuit

Adults enter the corridor.

They know it exists.

The crew runs.

The school itself becomes the pursuer because S.C.A.L.E. controls routes based on biological profiles.

Doors predict body temperature.
Climbing routes retract.
Water redirects.
Lights flood nocturnal paths.
Warm corridors cool before students reach them.

Ko'Bra realizes the problem:

**It knows how we move.**

Baskari stops.

Smiles.

**Then move wrong.**

The crew deliberately violates its own predictions.

Climbers stay grounded.
Warm-bodied students take cold routes.
Aquatic-adapted students choose dry paths.
Expected sensory behavior is scrambled.

For a few seconds S.C.A.L.E. loses them.

**PREDICTION FAILED**

Baskari:

**Scale that.**

## Episode ending

They escape to the roof.

Nobody is celebrating yet.

IRaKodi finally projects one recovered line:

**S.C.A.L.E. CHILD DEVELOPMENT TRIAL 01**

**STATUS: SUCCESSFUL**

Chroma looks back at the school.

**Successful for who?**

Cut to black.

## Episode 1 knowledge absorbed through story

The final script should naturally expose viewers to:

- ectothermy / behavioral thermoregulation;
- microclimates;
- species-specific environmental needs;
- sensory diversity;
- biological evidence vs interpretation;
- the difference between environmental measurement and human judgment.

Do not stop the story to define these terms unless a character genuinely needs the definition.
