1 · Game: Coast to the Line

REAL SCIENCE Momentum is p = m·v (mass × velocity). Hold PUSH (or hold the track) to kick. Let go to coast. Friction slows you down. Try to stop with your board's nose inside the green zone.

Board:
v = 0.00 m/s · p = 0 kg·m/s
Push, then release to coast.
Simplification: the push force and the friction force are the same for both boards. Real rolling friction grows with weight, so real results vary.
