1 · Opening game: Breath Wave

REALITY When you breathe out slower than you breathe in, your body's calming system (the parasympathetic nervous system) gets more active. Naming a feeling in words ("affect labeling") can also turn down a strong emotion a little. Neither one is magic. Both get better with practice.

Step 1: Tap the feeling closest to how you feel right now and how strong it is.

Step 2: Follow the circle for 4 rounds. Breathe in for 4 counts and out for 6.

4 · Field mission

PROPOSAL Daily 2-minute breath check + feeling log, for 7 days.

  1. Once a day, name your feeling and rate how strong it is (1–10).
  2. Do 2 minutes of breathing, in for 4 and out for 6.
  3. Name it and rate it again, then write one line about what happened.

Readiness check PROPOSAL: Can I name what I feel and calm myself? Can I explain one thing I'm curious about? Answer it yourself, then talk it over with a Man-stage mentor who listens without judging. You can pass, or you can choose to stay at this stage. Nobody moves you up under pressure.
