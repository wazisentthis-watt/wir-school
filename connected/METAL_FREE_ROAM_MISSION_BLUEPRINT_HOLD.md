⚙️🛹 METAL: IN DA RIFFT

The Free-Roam Mission Blueprint • Morrow City

I want Metal’s free-roam game to feel like you’re actually living through his movies and television series, not selecting episodes from a menu.

Every episode becomes a mission with its own villain, skating mechanics, neighborhood, cinematics, dialogue, and consequences. Some are enormous battles. Others are investigations, concerts, races, rescues, or ordinary days in Metal’s life.

And here’s the important part: you can still play the game between the episodes. Morrow City doesn’t stop existing when Metal finishes a mission.

🎬 Part 1: The movies become the opening campaign

Movie 0: Before Metal: Youth of Intsimbi Solano

Prequel campaign

You begin as young Intsimbi. No Galaxy Gallium. No transformation.

You learn to skate, navigate school and the neighborhoods, play instruments, meet friends, and experience the events that shape his life.

Final mission: A difficult period following the loss of his left lower leg, learning to live with his prosthetic, and rebuilding his relationship with skating.

Unlock: Human Metal’s movement foundation, music studio, early Morrow locations, and backstory.

Movie 1: RIFFT Rider: Second Thought

Origin campaign

Metal competes in skating events, faces pressure from the city, investigates FIRSTLINE and its artificial intelligences, and reaches Dead Man’s Pipe.

The fall becomes a playable cinematic survival sequence. Galaxy Gallium bonds with Metal, opening up completely new movement and physics systems.

The later missions introduce Gyre, Kairo, the Purple Deep, and Metal’s battle to protect human choice.

Final mission: Morrow City distributes the load together. The player must coordinate the city rather than simply overpower the enemy.

Unlock: RIFFT Up transformation, Gallium manipulation, Heavy Crown, and the full superhero free-roam experience.

📺 Part 2: Season One becomes 30 main missions

Each entry below is a proposed playable mission based on the archived 30-episode story bible.

Chapter 1: The streets teach Metal again

Episode / Mission	How the player experiences it
1. Concrete Has a Tempo	Skate to a neighborhood recording session. Chase Ballast after a depot robbery. Lose the first confrontation, save civilians beneath a collapsing market awning, then defeat him by manipulating the delivery platform instead of throwing a bigger punch.
2. The Bill Moves	Pursue Slipline through a transit district. Discover that your attacks transfer force into nearby buildings. Track the discharge pads and redirect her momentum into an evacuated crash lane.
3. Strong Is Not Tough	Investigate cracking apartment stairways. Scan and physically test building materials using board vibrations. Expose Glassjaw’s fraudulent reinforcement and trap him without collapsing the housing complex.
4. Temperature Geography	Fight Kiln in the foundry. Rails overheat, metal contracts, and Gallium changes behavior according to temperature. Navigate heat and cold zones to rescue workers and disable the thermal system.
5. The Street Hits Twice	An outdoor skate-and-dance party becomes a battlefield. Echo Nail’s spikes repeat impacts seven seconds later. You must time tricks, move civilians, and extract the spikes before the delayed hits return.
6. Four Wheels Down	Enter a youth skate event. Ballast and Slipline interrupt an armored delivery, trapping younger skaters. Win the rescue using ordinary skating first, then carefully activate Gallium to contain the villains.

Chapter reward: Better skate handling, board control challenges, local reputation, and six collected recording stems for Metal’s album.

Chapter 2: Control the route

Episode / Mission	How the player experiences it
7. Anchor Point	Clear a pedestrian bridge, identify structurally safe anchor points, and defeat Slipline by building a deliberate chain of momentum transfers.
8. False Strong	Investigate a Gallium repair that Metal himself made incorrectly. Repair it publicly while Glassjaw sabotages the surrounding structures.
9. Cold Side of Fire	Cross a district experiencing extreme thermal changes. Learn Gallium Gallop, chaining physical compression and release across roads, walls, pipes, and moving trams.
10. The Beat Comes Back	With Metal injured, explore a recording warehouse at night. Use echoes, careful board taps, and timing to locate trapped workers and discover Echo Nail’s hidden network.
11. East Is Down	Nyaxi manipulates magnetic forces across a transit avenue. The player’s board and left prosthetic respond differently. Learn to select real anchor relationships while the environment fights your movement.
12. Socket Weather	Defend a prosthetics clinic while Metal is injured. Skate through nonmagnetic paths, protect patients, and decide when to abandon pursuit to keep the clinic safe.

Chapter reward: Snapline route control, magnetic-environment challenges, prosthetic repair mechanics, and improved mission planning.

Chapter 3: Morrow is a machine

Episode / Mission	How the player experiences it
13. Loose Things	Patchwork causes Metal’s skateboard and prosthetic joints to loosen. Get to a safe workshop with limited control, then investigate sabotaged city equipment.
14. The Sound of a Failing Bolt	Perform at a music venue while investigating a dangerous support truss. Keep the crowd calm, reach the catwalk, and reinforce several failing joints at once.
15. What the Floor Knows	Enter an abandoned Gyre station. Rattle manipulates cables, counterweights, and false floors. This is a major boss encounter Metal is intended to lose, followed by an emergency extraction.
16. Gyre Lesson	Return to the station with Osei. Learn how loads travel through a structure, maneuver through rotating platforms, and escape without destroying the station.
17. Unpaid Weight	Fight Ballast at an apartment tower, then realize Load Shark has been sending the force of your attacks into occupied rooms. Switch immediately to a floor-by-floor rescue.
18. Every Floor Becomes a Ceiling	A major vertical boss battle against Load Shark. Rescue occupants, brace moving floors, trace hidden structural loads, and capture him. The tower still suffers permanent damage.

Chapter reward: Structural load sensing, advanced Gallium bracing, vertical traversal and major changes to the city map.

Chapter 4: The city fights back

Episode / Mission	How the player experiences it
19. Morrow Unplugged	Play through a district-wide blackout. You cannot rely on high Gallium output. Use radio reports from residents, manual navigation, and neighborhood teamwork to stop Kiln.
20. A City That Remembers	Investigate construction machinery moving without operators. The Foreman introduces Labor Ghosting: buildings and machines repeat historical work motions. Survive and evacuate as Metal exhausts his Gallium.
21. Side B	No supervillain. Spend a day recovering, repairing your board, visiting displaced families, recording music, and playing SKATE with Mari. Lil Heavy appears in a character-focused scene.
22. Route Reverse	Nyaxi and Patchwork attack Metal’s assembled systems. Your board partially comes apart. Recover its components through real physical contact and controlled movement.
23. The Honest Crack	Explore dangerous Gyre structures with Osei. Identify fake repairs, fight Glassjaw, and discover more of the Foreman’s structural-memory network.
24. Seven Seconds Later	Work reluctantly with Echo Nail to remove impact-memory devices across Morrow. In the finale, program a protective brace rather than a destructive strike into the repeating system.

Chapter reward: Limited repair assistance, neighborhood alliances, improved scanning and a clearer map of the Foreman’s network.

Chapter 5: Everybody out

Episode / Mission	How the player experiences it
25. Nobody Operating	Cross an industrial corridor while cranes, lifts, and machines repeat dangerous work cycles. Coordinate engineers and skaters to identify safe openings.
26. Catch the Floor	Load Shark threatens a residential rehabilitation center. Catch a collapsing floor with precise prosthetic and Gallium alignment while civilians evacuate. Ballast unexpectedly helps.
27. Assembly of One	Work alongside Patchwork to disassemble and rebuild a dangerous transit structure. Evade a Framekeeper whose classification system mistakenly targets Metal.
28. The Work Choir	Enter the original Gyre chamber. The Foreman replays the physical force of previous encounters. Reuse movement techniques learned throughout the season to survive.
29. Return Integrity	A citywide load crisis begins. Link real anchor points, use Heavy Metal RIFFT to shorten dangerous traversal routes, and recover your board and body alignment after every crossing. No teleportation.
30. Everybody Out	Season finale: The entire city becomes the mission. Switch between rescue routes, coordinating allies, stabilizing buildings, and disrupting the Foreman’s system. The ultimate win condition is getting people home, not maximizing enemy damage.

Final reward: The complete City Under My Wheels record, a transformed Morrow City, and a New Game+ option that preserves Metal’s earned abilities and opens repeatable city events.

🎮 Part 4: Bring the other Metal games into one world

We shouldn’t make players switch between 20 incompatible Metal builds. We should recover the best verified mechanic from each and give it a home inside the free-roam experience.

Skate missions

Bring in the strongest mechanics from Top-Down, Waymaker, Frameborn, and Skate Flow. Street competitions, trick lines, timed deliveries, balance challenges, and dynamic skate chases.

Superpower and combat missions

Recover swinging, grabbing, finite Gallium matter, transformation, and destruction mechanics from Swing Gallium, Gorillium, Morrow Alive and the 3D Test Yard experiments.

Driving and racing missions

Connect ReFramer Rush and FaFiFi Racing. Let Metal ride motorcycles, drive tuned cars, compete in Midnight Club-style races, and eventually enter cross-reality driving events.

Stealth and crossover missions

Metal, Savannah Spider, Lil Heavy and SLOTTA-related encounters become optional cooperative stories, heists and character-specific challenges.

Sports and everyday-life missions

Include Metal’s Gridiron Dynasty appearances as a connected sports activity. Add neighborhood basketball, skate tournaments, music recording, shopping, repairs, and hanging out with friends.

🏙️ Part 5: How free roaming should actually function

The most important thing is that missions change the city even after you finish them.

For example, after Episode 18, a damaged apartment tower should still be evacuated and under repair. Workers should occupy the street. Residents should discuss what happened. Metal can voluntarily return to help, even after completing that story mission.

Here are the systems I would build into the entire game:

* Natural mission discovery: Calls from friends, emergencies visible from rooftops, radio broadcasts, NPC conversations, and events occurring as you skate.
* Playable transformations: Metal stays human until you activate RIFFT Up. Some missions actually work better without powers.
* No giant mobile-app HUD: A clean video-game interface, with contextual prompts, diegetic indicators, and minimal on-screen clutter.
* Physical combat: Enemies react to forces, collisions, grabs, momentum, and the environment. Damage is not just a health bar disappearing.
* Reactive NPCs: Civilians evacuate, police respond, enemies adapt, shops reopen, and friends remember your choices.
* Permanent mission consequences: Damaged boards, Gallium depletion, prosthetic maintenance, structural repairs, neighborhood trust, and changing city conditions.
* Multiple ways to finish: Capture the villain, rescue everyone, prevent environmental damage, or sometimes retreat to avoid a worse disaster.

🛠️ Where I’d start development

First complete playable mission: Concrete Has a Tempo

The vertical slice that establishes the standard for every future episode

* Spawn as human Metal at his neighborhood skate spot.
* Skate through a real, connected section of Morrow City.
* Encounter Ballast’s depot robbery without loading a separate arena.
* Experience the first defeat and a physically simulated awning collapse.
* Rescue civilians, transform, and defeat Ballast through environmental physics.
* Return to the recording session, save progress, and continue free roaming.

Once that loop is working reliably with controller, keyboard, and touch input, the remaining episodes can reuse the same mission framework while introducing their own physics and storytelling.

The overall vision: One Morrow City, two major opening story campaigns, 30 Season One missions, additional episode arcs, and all the best gameplay we have developed across Metal’s different versions.

The big difference from a traditional superhero game is that Metal isn’t just protecting Morrow City. He’s growing up inside it, making music from it, breaking things in it, repairing them, and learning how much of its weight he can really carry. ⚙️🛹
