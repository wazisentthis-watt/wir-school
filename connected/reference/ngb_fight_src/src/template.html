PHATOM PMK · BODY CONTROL
Your body is the controller

  
Hands, guard, slips, ducks, twist and punches come from your body. A controller (if connected) keeps your feet, cooking and pause. Camera frames never leave this device.

  
  • Guard — gloves by your face block for real.
  • Punch — throw it. Jab, cross, hooks, uppercuts, overhands and body shots read from how your fist travels. Stopping short is a feint.
  • Hold it out — your arm stays extended (and you're open to counters).
  • Slip / duck / lean back / roll — move your head and shoulders.
  • Power — turn your hips and shoulders into it.
  • Clinch — reach both hands out slowly, close together. Pull one back to free it for dirty boxing.
  • Feet — controller left stick, or step left/right, toward/away from the camera. With your knees and ankles in the picture: lengthen your stance = scoot the rear foot back (A), bring the right foot up = B, turn on your planted left foot clockwise = pivot (X), on your right foot counter-clockwise = pivot (Y), and physically switch your lead foot to switch stance.
  • Both gloves high over your head 1 s — pause.

  
Phone: 2–3 m away with your whole body in the shot — in front of you OR off to the side like filming your bag work (portrait or landscape both work). The two calibration punches tell the game which way you're facing. Must be opened from an https:// link in Safari/Chrome.
