2 · The recipe

How the Glazed Ring fighters are built
  1. Skeleton from real mocap. The rig is the Mixamo skeleton from AMukkani's boxing FBX files, so every captured punch, slip and knockdown plays on it.
  2. Bodies grown from code, not sculpted. Each body part is a loft: rings of superellipse cross-sections stacked along a bone and stitched into triangles.
  3. Muscle as math. Ring radius changes along the limb and around it (biceps on the front, triceps on the back), so the shape reads as muscle.
  4. Skin weights while building. Every vertex knows which bones move it at the moment it is made, blended across joints.
  5. Materials painted by code. Canvas-drawn colour, bump and glow maps (noodle fibres, graham dots, popcorn bubbles, cast-iron cracks) on physical materials with sheen and clearcoat.
  6. One recipe per fighter. Body proportions, skin kind, hair style and colours live in one roster entry; the Forge turns that idea into an open JSON recipe any AI can write.
