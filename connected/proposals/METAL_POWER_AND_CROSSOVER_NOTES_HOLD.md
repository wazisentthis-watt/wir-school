# METAL Power & Crossover Proposal Notes
**WIR Books / WIR Connected proposal only · NOT locked canon or official crossover license.**

## METAL: Galaxy RIFFT Beast design
The advanced appearance experiments with a lion Crown, gorilla leverage, six octopus/Gallium limbs, black and violet galaxy-metal, and broken musical cords integrated into the suit. Continue to preserve Metal's established face-coverage/mask rules, Afro/Crown continuity, left prosthetic and four-wheel board. The reference visuals are art-development candidates, not automatic approvals.

### Design pillars
1. **Independent six-tentacle behavior:** Guard, Counter, Anchor, Grapple, Mobility, Rescue, plus hand-directed commands. One limb can protect a civilian while another secures an enemy.
2. **Finite Galaxy Gallium:** allocate armor/limbs/board/constructions, track material loss and 80%-efficiency reclaim as a prototype constant.
3. **Physics-aware leverage:** grounded Gorilla Load, safe anchor stress and redirected momentum with consequences. No energy creation.
4. **Skating as locomotion:** four-wheel board, ollie/kickflip/grind, supported ramps and Gallium Gallop push. No hover or teleport shortcut.
5. **Snap neural timing:** brief precision and counter window, Focus drain, Heat rise and Snap Storm overload.
6. **Crown scanning + Broken Chord resonance:** structural weaknesses and timing patterns; environmental effects tied to susceptible materials, not infinite unbounded magic.
7. **RIFFT crossings:** source/destination surface quality, route length, Return integrity and recovery, spatial motion rather than unconditional instant teleport.
8. **Lil Heavy:** autonomous tiny four-wheel board companion; original WIR notes favor eight small appendages, a gameplay design whose exact book-canon status is still under review.
9. **Cold Lock / Hot Line:** trade rigid defense/heat management against high-output adaptable strikes.
10. **Crown Dominion:** six coordinated limbs and prepared anchors, verified scan, resonance, heat/focus/Gallium costs, three-beat wind-up → tension → initiation.

## Speculative three-world Zukana storyline
Three young characters with Melanitez-derived ancestral inheritance blended with native world powers:
- **Veyra N’Korum, 19, Koruva:** gravitropic Koravari world of drifting landmasses; Melanitez-derived force-bond anchors allow redistribution of load. Cost: load path/support failure.
- **Ozi’Kalon, 18, Veshkara:** living crystal planet with Veshkin architectural bodies; Continuance-derived scaffold structures bridge limited spatial seams. Cost: strain/structure energy.
- **Neya’Tsil, 17, Oranthi:** Orathi predict near-future branches; inherited signal/memory capabilities examine residual traces of damaged events. Cost: sensory overload and conflicting histories.
Their powers are complementary but do **not** make them inherently stronger than each world's veteran native champions. Origins, names, lineages, abilities and relationship to canon remain proposals.
