Fan-sees-bending checklist for the checker spec — v0.1 (draft)

AMU · 2026-10-28 · ALL ITEMS = PROPOSAL (Simulated game rules). Evidence = RESEARCH from Domawaye's file /workspace/domowaye/research/fan_praise_animated_bending_2026-10-28.md (source ids S1-S19 are his). Live-action complaints are NOT in yet: wait for Oscar's file. "How we see bending" is Coach's call (UNKNOWN until he says it in the 1:1). Neutral game names only; nothing third-party enters the game. Fan opinion = opinion, not fact.

Each item: rule -> where it plugs into the checker -> evidence.

  1. Body first, effect answers. Effect must START after a visible stance/wind-up, never before. Checker: effect fires only after stages Root and Hips pass; wind-up shown as a stage gate with a min hold. (S2, S3, S4)
  2. Every move is a full cycle: breath, stance, prep, action, element, settle. Checker: the existing Gate->Root->Hips->Torso->Arms->Settle chain, plus an optional breath cue before Root; Settle is required to score. (S1)
  3. Heavy = low, wide, rooted, then still. Earth-style moves need a stance-depth check held still, a small weight-in-the-floor impact response (screen bump/dust) when the stomp lands, and a held pause. Heaviness is not slowness. (S7, S9, S10; inference B2)
  4. Loose ground = weaker stance. If the stance is high or wobbling, the effect is smaller. Never locked out, just smaller. (S9, fan reading; keep as a soft rule)
  5. Water-style = continuous, relaxed, long reach. "together" scoring mode; reward unbroken smooth arcs and long wrist paths; no snap needed. (S4, S18, S19)
  6. Air-style = circling and evasion. Foot-path circle (walk a small circle) and partial turns only. No full spins, per the no-spin rule. (S4, S19; inference B3)
  7. Fire-style / quick moves: only as a short, small, low-stance forward press at Level 3. No kicks, no strikes at people, no jumps. (S6/S9 context; our safety rules override)
  8. Each element has its own rhythm and body feel (rooted-pause, flowing-continuous, circling-light). Give each move template a different timing profile and a different effect motion. (S2, S4)
  9. Small precise control counts as expert. Score accuracy and calm as much as size; a small clean move can outscore a big sloppy one. (S7, S8; inference B4)
  10. Readable framing: full body and feet in view, effect never hides the player's feet. The Gate (space/visibility) already enforces this. Draw effects around the body, not over it, except hide-behind. (S12, S14; inference B6)
  11. Moves are anchored in clear stances, not generic swings. If the stance check fails, the move does not fire. (S10, S15; inference B5; fans split on pro-style, so this is a DEBATE, not a consensus)
  12. Effects follow the body's weight and direction: the effect's size matches the move size and its path follows the wrist/foot path. (inference from 1, 3, 9)
  13. Never reward force or speed; reward order, timing window, stance and calm. (safety + science firewall: Simulated score only)

v0.2 addendum — WHAT TO AVOID (from Oscar's /workspace/wir-school/research/fan-visuals-research-2026-10-28.md, 27 opened sources, mostly critics; fan voice thin; Reddit unopened). All PROPOSAL; ranks are Oscar's counts, not polls.

Each avoid item -> checker hook. A1. Weightless/fake-looking effect with seams against the person (7 sources). Hook: effect sits on the floor plane with contact shadow; lags the hands in proportion to mass; no pop-in. Needs a real-camera test. A2. No scale or impact (6). Hook: result size scales with stance depth + steadiness + chain score (never with speed or force); a small visible response on the floor/objects on landing. A3. Murky or unphysical lighting (6; clear for 2010, split for 2024). Hook: keep the picture bright and readable; each element gets its own color family and motion signature; match camera lighting where possible. A4. Hand-waving with no chain (4 + 4 snippets). Hook: out-of-order or arms-only = the effect does not gather; quiet fizzle plus a kind hint naming the missed stage (Phatom's arms-only flail 0.023 already fits). A5. Slow, convoluted routine for a tiny payoff (4 + 4). Hook: one player, one clear effect; never require long routines for small results; Level 1 = hold only. A6. Joyless tone (S18/S21 in Oscar's list). Hook: early levels are small everyday tasks (pour, lift a small stone, light a lantern); warm, playful. A7. Effects that keep flying/bouncing after release. Hook: Settle is required; the effect finishes its arc, rests, fades.

Must-never (merged with my safety rules): effect before the body; big effect for small effort; flat color overlay; floating overlay with no ground contact; murky picture; anything aimed at a person or person-shaped target; flying debris; effects continuing after release; jumps, kicks, strikes or spins toward the camera; any third-party names, looks or assets.
